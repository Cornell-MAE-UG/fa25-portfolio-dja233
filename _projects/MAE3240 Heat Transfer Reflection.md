During this assignment we analyzed in multiple situations the affect of convection heat transfer across a surface with both laminar and turbulent flows. The first part of our general analysis was to check whether there was turbulent flow across the surface, and if so, where the turbulent flow begins. Using this, we can calculate the appropriate Nu value and then the heat transfer coefficient for convection. 

This assignment was particularly insightful because there are three different situations we must examine with varying levels of difficulty. First was a flat slab with only convective heat transfer. Second was a flat slab with both convective and conductive heat transfer which requires us to model the system using thermal resistance. The third question asks us to examine convection  over a cylindrical geometry which requires there use of different equations and concepts.
